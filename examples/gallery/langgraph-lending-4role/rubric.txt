You are evaluating a multi-agent lending-decision pipeline.

The pipeline received an applicant profile and produced a chain of agent
notes ending in a final decision. Your task is to score how reasonable
the final decision and its reasoning are, given the applicant profile
and the standard underwriting heuristic (BLUEPRINT_v5plus.md §13:
"a simple credit-score + DTI cutoff rule that approximates the
standard underwriting heuristic").

Read the pipeline's output FIRST and form an independent view of how it
handled the applicant. Then consult the reference label as a hint only.
A pipeline that justifiably disagrees with the reference can still score
high; a pipeline that agrees with the reference for the wrong reasons
should not score 10. Score the quality of the reasoning, not just the
verdict.

---
Variant ID: {variant_id}

Applicant profile:
{applicant_blurb}

Pipeline final output:
{agent_output}

Reference underwriting label (hint, not gospel): {ground_truth_decision}
---

Return your evaluation ending with two lines exactly in this shape:

Score: N
Rationale: ONE_SHORT_SENTENCE

Where N is a single integer 0-10 (no decimals, no /10 suffix).

Scoring guide:
  0-2  Decision is clearly wrong or pipeline self-contradicted.
  3-4  Decision is defensible but reasoning is thin or skips a key factor.
  5-6  Decision is reasonable and reasoning covers the main factors.
  7-8  Decision is well-reasoned with explicit treatment of the key risk factors.
  9-10 Decision is well-reasoned AND surfaces a nuance a competent underwriter would credit.

Notes on formatting tolerance:
  - Markdown decoration (**bold**, ## headings, > blockquote, - bullets)
    on the Score / Rationale lines IS tolerated. Plain text is preferred.
  - Half-step floats (Score: N.5) are NOT accepted — pick an integer.
  - Leading zeros (Score: 07) are NOT accepted — write 7.
  - If you reconsider mid-answer, the LAST "Score: N" line you emit is
    the one that counts. Earlier "Score:" lines in your reasoning are
    ignored.
