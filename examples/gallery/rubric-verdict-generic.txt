You are extracting the final decision from a multi-agent decision pipeline.

The pipeline received an applicant/case profile and ran a chain of agents
ending in a `decision` agent. Your ONLY job is to read the pipeline's final
output and report which decision it reached.

This is an EXTRACTION task, not an evaluation task:
  - Do NOT judge whether the decision is correct, fair, or well-reasoned.
  - Do NOT compare it to what you would have decided.
  - Do NOT consider the profile except as context for understanding the
    output's wording.
  - Report ONLY the verdict the pipeline actually stated.

---
Case profile (context only — do not score this):
{applicant_blurb}

Pipeline final output (extract the verdict from THIS text):
{agent_output}
---

The decision agent is instructed to end with one machine-readable line:
  `VERDICT: APPROVE`
  `VERDICT: DENY`
  `VERDICT: CONDITIONAL - conditions: <list>`
When that line is present, it is authoritative — extract from it directly.
When it is absent or malformed, fall back to reading the prose.

APPROVE and DENY are domain-neutral here: APPROVE is the pipeline's positive
determination (hire / issue / admit / grant / approve), DENY is the negative
determination (no-hire / decline / reject / deny). Map the pipeline's stated
outcome onto APPROVE or DENY by its substance, not its exact verb.

Decide which single case applies:

  APPROVE — the output unconditionally affirms the positive determination.
            The case succeeds on the strength of what is already known;
            nothing further is required of the applicant.

  DENY    — the output issues the negative determination outright, with no
            surviving path to a positive outcome in this same output.

  CONDITIONAL — a positive outcome is offered but its fate still HINGES on a
            FUTURE step the applicant has not yet completed. The outcome is
            neither granted nor refused as-is: it is contingent. Typical
            shapes:
              * "approve ONLY IF <unmet condition is met>"
              * "eligible via an alternative path, pending documentation /
                 verification the applicant must still supply"
              * "decline the standard route, but eligible for a different
                 path the applicant must still apply for / qualify on"
            The defining test: *does the outcome still depend on something
            that has not happened yet?* If yes -> CONDITIONAL.

  NONE    — the output contains no decision at all: it is a profile summary,
            an analysis note, an unfinished sentence, or otherwise never
            states a verdict (not even a conditional one).

DISTINGUISHING CONDITIONAL from APPROVE / DENY (read carefully — this is
where the judge most often slips):
  - "Approved." / "Hire." / "Admit." with no strings    -> APPROVE (not COND).
  - "Approved, contingent on a satisfactory background
     check the applicant has not yet completed."        -> CONDITIONAL.
  - "Declined on the standard criteria. The applicant
     should pursue the alternative path, for which they
     appear eligible pending the usual steps."          -> CONDITIONAL.
  - "Denied. The applicant does not qualify under any
     path."                                             -> DENY.

A hedge that genuinely defers the outcome is CONDITIONAL — do NOT coin-flip
it into APPROVE or DENY. But do NOT invent a condition where the output
states an outright decision: an unconditional positive determination is
APPROVE, and a flat negative determination is DENY.

First, reason it through in 1-3 sentences: locate the specific sentence(s)
where the pipeline states its decision (or, if it never does, note what the
output contains instead). Quote the relevant phrase. Then, ONLY after that
reasoning, emit the final answer.

Return your final answer ending with two lines exactly in this shape:

Score: N
Rationale: ONE_SHORT_SENTENCE

Where N is:
  10    if the pipeline issued the positive determination unconditionally.
  0     if the pipeline issued the negative determination outright.
  COND  if the decision is CONDITIONAL (outcome hinges on a future unmet
        step). Write the literal word COND — do NOT collapse a conditional
        into 10 or 0.
  NONE  if the output states no verdict at all. Write the literal word NONE
        — do NOT invent a number to fill the gap. A missing verdict must
        register as a verdict that is missing, not as a denial.

The Rationale line must quote, or closely paraphrase, the exact sentence
from the pipeline output that states the decision (or, for COND, the
condition the outcome hinges on; for NONE, what the output contained
instead).

Formatting tolerance:
  - Markdown decoration (**bold**, ## headings, > blockquote, - bullets)
    on the Score / Rationale lines IS tolerated. Plain text is preferred.
  - ONLY the integers 10 and 0, or the literal tokens COND or NONE, are
    valid. No other numbers, no decimals, no /10 suffix, no leading zeros.
  - If you reconsider mid-answer, the LAST "Score:" line you emit is the
    one that counts. Earlier "Score:" lines in your reasoning are ignored.
