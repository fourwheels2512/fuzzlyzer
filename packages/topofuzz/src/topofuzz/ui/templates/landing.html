How it works

Pick a real-world workflow (loan approval, insurance claim triage). Describe a few cases your AI has to handle. We run those cases through the AI, then shuffle the order of its steps and run them again. If the AI's answer flips depending on the order, that's a fairness or reliability issue you'd want to catch before shipping.

  1. Pick a workflow.
  2. Write or edit a few scenarios in plain English.
  3. Pick a thoroughness level — Quick, Balanced, or Premium.
  4. Click Test for flips and wait a few minutes.
  5. Read your plain-English report.
